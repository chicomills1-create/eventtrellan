THE DETAILS

A few things worth knowing.

Can I sell tickets through the demo?

No. The demo is a design and messaging sandbox. Production ticketing needs a payment/ticketing integration and reviewed policies.

Will my invitations send automatically?

Not in the prototype. Actual messages require an authorized sender, recipient consent and connected email/SMS systems.

Is this an event planner?

This is a launch-asset service, not physical event coordination, staffing, insurance or venue booking.

Can an event page have my domain?

The published product can support custom-domain routing after domain ownership and hosting are verified.
