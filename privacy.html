Privacy Policy

EventTrellan is a service of Apex Grid Engineering PLLC.

What we collect

Account and order details (name, email, payment confirmation handled by our payment processor — we do not store card numbers), your event brief (the event facts you supply), your confirmation records (brief and proof versions, your approval, and timestamps, kept as the delivery record for your event), and messages you send us.

How we use it

Only to build, confirm, publish, and support your event kit, to send you the acknowledgments, deliveries, and service messages your order requires, and to keep the accuracy and delivery records this service is sold on. We do not sell personal information. We do not use your event facts, or any guest information, to market EventTrellan or any other company to your guests — your guests are never our audience.

Guest information

At launch we do not collect or retain guest lists. If you supply any guest-related information strictly to prepare your materials, it is used only for that preparation and deleted or exported to you on request.

Sharing

We share data only with the processors needed to run the service (hosting, email delivery, payments), under their own security terms, and when required by law.

Retention and your rights

We keep order and confirmation records for the period needed for accounting, disputes, and accuracy audits. You may request export or deletion of your personal data at any time at info@eventtrellan.com; deletion does not erase records we must keep by law.
