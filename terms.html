Terms of Service

Effective on the launch of paid service. EventTrellan is a service of Apex Grid Engineering PLLC.

1. What EventTrellan is

EventTrellan creates launch assets for a single event — a microsite, invitation copy, a reminder plan, and promotional copy — from facts you supply. EventTrellan is not an event planner or coordinator. We do not book venues, hire staff or vendors, provide insurance, sell tickets, or guarantee attendance, venue availability, or any event outcome.

2. Your facts and the confirmation rule

Every date, time, time zone, place, address, host name, RSVP instruction, and other event detail we publish comes from your brief. Before publication we show the details back to you; publication happens only after your explicit confirmation, which we record with the brief version, the proof version, and a timestamp. If your inputs conflict or appear mistaken, we will ask you to resolve them — we never silently change your facts. Changes after publication require your confirmation of the changed fields, and change fees, if any, are shown to you before you authorize additional work.

3. Orders, tiers, and delivery

Each order covers one event at the tier you select (Essentials, Launch, or Signature). Before checkout we display what the tier includes: deliverables and quantities, included revisions (one proof cycle and one consolidated revision round), the support window, the page-hosting period, the post-event expiration and export process, and the minimum event lead time. Orders placed inside the lead-time requirement may be declined or refunded where we cannot responsibly fulfill them.

4. Sending and guest data

At launch, EventTrellan prepares invitation and reminder materials for you to distribute through your own channels. We do not upload, retain, market to, or send messages to your guest lists. Your event facts and any guest information you supply are yours; we do not share them or use them for any other brand's marketing.

5. Refunds

Refunds remedy our failure, not consumed value. An order cancelled before drafting begins is refunded in full. Once drafting has begun, fees for work actually performed — as disclosed at checkout — are earned, and refunds cover only the undelivered portion. Our remedy ladder is: re-perform or fix, then account credit, then refund as a last resort. If EventTrellan introduces or publishes a material error that was not in your approved information, we will notify you promptly, correct or disable the affected publication, prepare a guest correction message for your approval, and assess the affected deliverables; if the error materially defeats the purpose of the affected service and cannot reasonably be cured, you will receive an appropriate refund for the affected service, up to the full event-order amount where warranted, subject to applicable law. A chargeback pauses only the disputed work while we respond with the delivery and confirmation records; your undisputed data and exports remain accessible. We may decline future service for documented fraud, misuse, or repeated abusive disputes.

6. Acceptable use

You confirm that you have the right to use the images, logos, and facts you supply, and that your event and its promotion comply with applicable law. We may decline or remove work that is unlawful, deceptive, or infringes another's rights.

7. Liability

To the extent permitted by law, our liability for a paid order is limited to the amount you paid for that order. We are not liable for the success, attendance, or conduct of your event.
