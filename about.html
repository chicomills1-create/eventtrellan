ABOUT EVENTTRELLAN

Every event deserves an entrance.

EventTrellan builds the launch kit for one event at a time: a microsite, invitations, a reminder plan, and promo copy — every detail confirmed by the host before anything goes live.

HOW WE WORK

Your facts. Confirmed, then published.

Every date, time, time zone, place, and detail on an EventTrellan page comes from the host's own brief and is shown back for explicit confirmation before publication. We never invent a detail and never silently correct one — if something looks off, we ask. EventTrellan is a launch-asset service, not an event planner: we do not book venues, staff events, sell tickets, or guarantee attendance.
